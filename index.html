Private local backend.

This publishing tool runs on Mikita Plotnikau’s computer and is not offered as a public website or service. It has no public sign-up, user accounts, or public login page. The public pages here document the tool and its data handling; they do not provide access to the backend.

What it does

Mikita Plotnikau uses the local tool for channels he owns: Ad Scriptorium, Hebrew Dialogues, and Animal Calm. It publishes and updates videos, metadata, localized titles and descriptions, thumbnails, and caption tracks. Publishing is initiated from a local command-line interface; the local studio shows preparation and publication status. Audio dubbing files are uploaded separately in YouTube Studio.

Access

There is no public endpoint for the backend. Access is limited to the owner on the local computer. Authentication with Google is performed through Google’s OAuth consent flow; the tool does not ask for or store Google account passwords.
